What the output CSV contains

All your original columns, unchanged and in order, plus: serp_provider, serp_query, serp_status, serp_error, serp_fetched_at, result_count, r1_title … r10_title, r1_link … r10_link, r1_snippet … r10_snippet, answer_box, knowledge_graph, people_also_ask, related_searches, top_stories, raw_json (the full API response for that row), and — with SerpApi — ai_overview_status (inline / lazy-loaded / none / error), ai_overview_text (the full AI Overview, word for word) and ai_overview_sources (the links it cites). With Serper the AI Overview columns stay empty.
